# Cite This

<p class="lede">Cite the instruments, not this site. The site is a reading aid; the definitions are published on Zenodo.</p>

## The family

To cite the Foundational Definitions as they currently stand, across all editions, use the **concept DOI**:

> **10.5281/zenodo.22181028** · <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22181028>

To pin the edition this site is built on, use the **version DOI** of the November 2026 Edition:

> T. Roxey, ed., *FD — Foundational Definitions, November 2026 Edition*, Eclectic Technologies, October 2026. <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23401272>

Earlier editions remain citable for what they contain. The October 2026 Edition is [10.5281/zenodo.23117612](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23117612), the September 2026 Edition is [10.5281/zenodo.23000686](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23000686), and the August 2026 Edition is [10.5281/zenodo.22181029](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22181029).

## Citing a section

Name the instrument and the section, for example *The Bright Line* §4.2, in *FD — Foundational Definitions, November 2026 Edition*. The bare citation form, by designation and section without a version token, resolves against FD-REG, *The Governing Stack Register*, which holds the version state of the family.
